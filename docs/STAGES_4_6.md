# Stages 4-6 — decision, validation and paper observation

## 4 Decision intelligence
A deterministic confluence layer combines structure, Order Flow, regime and macro evidence. Risk has an independent veto. LONG/SHORT require a threshold; otherwise WAIT. Probability remains unavailable until empirical OOS calibration.

## 5 Validation
Adds evidence reports and expanding walk-forward folds. Reports include sample size, expectancy, profit factor, max drawdown and a Sharpe-like per-trade statistic. Existing replay/backtest infrastructure remains the execution foundation.

A passing report is an evidence gate, not a profitability promise. Defaults retain the project policy of at least 100 paper/OOS trades, positive expectancy and PF >= 1.2.

## 6 Paper observation
Adds a timestamped paper ledger for decisions and reference prices. Delayed feeds can exercise research/paper-observation logic but are never live eligible. Actual continuous paper collection starts only when a market-data adapter is connected.

No broker orders and no real money.
