# Pre-data core — blocks 9–12

## 9. Forward outcome labelling
Creates forward-return, MAE/MFE and target/stop labels from future paths. Labels are generated only for research after the observation point and must never leak into live features.

## 10. Baseline challenge
Every candidate can be compared with a baseline. Positive candidate expectancy alone is not enough; uplift over the reference process must be measured.

## 11. Performance guard
Recent paper/live-like evidence fails closed when the sample is too small or recent expectancy falls below the configured floor.

## 12. Promotion orchestrator
Combines maturity, licensed-feed evidence, verified risk controls, explicit live authorization, kill-switch readiness and recent performance. Missing evidence keeps live execution blocked.

These blocks add governance and evidence plumbing. They do not establish profitability or calibrated probabilities.
